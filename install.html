About 10 minutes. Nothing to install and no code to edit.

1. Connect your accounts

Netlify runs the site. Supabase holds its data. Both have free plans. These tokens are used while you're on this page and are not saved.

You become the admin. Anyone with an email at this domain can ask to join, so it must be on your company's own domain, not Gmail or Outlook. It doesn't need Google Workspace. No company domain? See CUSTOMIZE.md in your new GitHub repo.
